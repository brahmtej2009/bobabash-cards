How it works

  1. Grab a card at the desk. It has your card number and a 4-digit PIN.
  2. Set it up on your laptop. Card number and PIN from your card, the email you gave at the desk, and a password. That's it, you're in.
  3. Point it anywhere. Your project page, a profile with your links, or any site. Pick what your card says while it loads, too.
  4. Tap it on any phone. Boba Bash logo for a moment, then straight to your page.

What you can do

  • Send your card to your project, your profile or any link
  • Build a profile page: photo, bio, links, socials, six themes
  • Write the line people see under the logo while it loads
  • See how many times it was tapped, from where, on what phone
  • Download your card's QR code
  • Ship your site to the sites repo and track your PR

Links stay locked during the event and open after. Lost your password? Reset it by email or with your card's PIN.
